Challenges and Required Support

The Fund’s activated key performance indicators for fiscal year 2025, corresponding to 1446–1447 AH.

  • Axes:
  • 01 Legislative / Regulatory Challenges
  • 02 Financial Challenges
  • 03 Procedural / Operational Challenges
01
First Axis

Legislative / Regulatory Challenges

Challenge No.

01.1

Align support programs with economic changes to ensure sustainability

Main Background/Justification: Changes in financing and market costs require flexibility to ensure sustainability and direct support to eligible segments.

Solution

Conduct periodic reviews of the support matrix and targeting frameworks and escalate required adjustments to the government.

Impact

Deliver more efficient and sustainable support with better targeting for eligible groups.

Decision / Required Support

Align and update entitlement rules, the support matrix, and support limits in line with economic changes.

Type of Decision

Standardize contract forms, clarify obligations, and establish guidance or approval policies across the financing system.

Responsible for Providing Support

Housing system

Complex contractual obligations and multiple parties (Fund–financier–beneficiary)

Main Background/Justification: Multiple parties increase the risk of discrepancy and disputes; unifying requirements reduces complaints and raises transparency.

Solution

Standardize contract templates and strengthen the clarity of obligations, notification, and investigation procedures.

Impact

Reduce disputes, increase transparency, and smooth the beneficiary journey.

Decision / Required Support

Unify minimum contract, disclosure, and compliance requirements among all financiers and participants.

Type of Decision

Regulation / unified financing market standard.

Responsible for Providing Support

Housing system financial partners, Saudi Central Bank

Challenge No.

02
Second Axis

Financial Challenges

Challenge No.

02.1

Volatility of financing costs and its impact on purchasing power

Main Background/Justification: Volatility in financing costs may weaken the ability to own; facilities preserve purchasing power and reduce dropout.

Solution

Design graduated/flexible support tools, activate financing partnerships, and improve targeting.

Impact

Enhance the ability to afford housing costs and reduce beneficiaries’ sensitivity to rising financing costs.

Decision / Required Support

Provide solutions that ease the financing burden (product design/graduated support) and suitable financing facilities for eligible groups.

Type of Decision

Sector-level financing arrangements/packages (external).

Responsible for Providing Support

Housing system, financing partners of the housing system

Credit default risks for some segments

Main Background/Justification: Strengthen credit risk management tools and ensure the sustainability of mortgage financing.

Solution

Activate guarantee tools under regulatory frameworks to enhance financing feasibility and reduce risks.

Impact

Achieve higher inclusion with risk control and improved portfolio quality.

Decision / Required Support

Support and enable guarantee tools to enhance financing feasibility and reduce risks.

Type of Decision

Regulation/enhancement of credit risk management controls.

Responsible for Providing Support

Housing system

Challenge No.

03
Third Axis

Procedural / Operational Challenges

Challenge No.

03.1

Difficulty in accelerating the beneficiary journey and reducing service completion time

Main Background/Justification: Reducing operational friction requires integration among parties to expedite procedures and improve service quality.

Solution

Expand digital channels and services, simplify procedures, and increase commitment to service levels.

Impact

Improve beneficiary experience and increase operational productivity.

Decision / Required Support

Enable digital integration, data exchange, and operational linkage with partners to reduce processing time and unify service levels.

Type of Decision

Participatory enablement / integration among system entities (external).

Responsible for Providing Support

Relevant government and digital authorities, financing partners, and technical partners (integration and SLA alignment).

Real Estate Development Fund

Enabling the growth of the Saudi housing market through innovative and sustainable financing solutions.
