Services Provided to Support Financing Products and Their Governance

REDF provides various technical support services to assist financing products and financing entities. Most notable are the construction progress monitoring reports for the Self Build product, real estate appraisal services, and housing eligibility reports for readymade units. In 2025, the financing products governance program contributed to enhancing the reliability of the real estate financing ecosystem and strengthening the trust of beneficiaries, financing entities, and developers by tightening the control of appraisal processes before finalising financing. During the year, 241,300 appraisal requests were completed, bringing the total number of requests since the program launch to 1,402,451. The services provided by REDF have contributed to raising the quality and improving the outcomes of real estate and technical appraisal reports, allowing beneficiaries to review the housing condition in advance and reinforcing partners' confidence in the sustainability of financing decisions.
