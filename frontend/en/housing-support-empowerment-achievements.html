Enhancing Social Stability for Women

The tangible positive impact of key support programs is evident in enhancing social stability for women, particularly divorcees and widows who serve as the primary breadwinners for their families. Through an integrated ecosystem of initiatives, including the Guarantee Program, Easy Mortgage Program, Profit Reduction Program, and In-Kind Support, approximately 32.02% of the total cumulative support has been directed toward divorcees, while widows have benefited from approximately 7.19% the total support.
