Conclusion

A look back at REDF's 2025 — empowering citizens to own their homes,
and supporting the housing ecosystem under Vision 2030.

Conclusion text

The annual report has set out a comprehensive account of the REDF's performance in 2025. This includes its contribution to the Housing Program and its role as a central steward of the Kingdom's housing finance ecosystem under Vision 2030.

Throughout 2025, REDF made meaningful progress toward the Vision 2030 homeownership target of 70%, with national ownership reaching 66.2%. This achievement reflects a more integrated, collaborative and financially sustainable housing system.

The year confirmed that homeownership is no longer treated solely as a social program, but as a cornerstone of economic participation, household stability, urban development and long term national prosperity.

The successes of 2025 were underpinned by three mutually reinforcing drivers: financial ecosystem leadership through deepened collaboration across the housing ecosystem; implementation of the updated corporate strategy and human capital transformation; and institutional excellence demonstrated through advanced digital transformation, automation, governance and increased data-driven decision making.

At the same time, the year also highlighted important challenges. Evolving demographic expectations, urbanisation pressures and the need to serve more vulnerable segments reinforced the importance of close alignment with the private sector, more responsive delivery mechanisms, and a sharper focus on sustainable support models — balancing wider access with the long-term financial sustainability of the system.

Building on these results, REDF will continue to advance its strategic initiatives, strengthen its financial sustainability, and develop innovative financing solutions, contributing to:

higher national homeownership, improved quality of life for citizens, and a future where REDF plays a leading role across the housing ecosystem, supporting vibrant and sustainable communities for generations to come.

Building lasting impact
and sustainability for the future of housing
