Corporate Strategy

Vision

Empowering and enhancing the growth of the Saudi housing market by providing sustainable and innovative financial solutions for citizens and market participants.

Mission

Increasing the percentage of homeownership among citizens by providing innovative and financially sustainable solutions for citizens and market participants.

Introduction

REDF was established by Royal Decree No. M/23 dated 11/06/1394, corresponding to 01/07/1974. It is organisationally linked to the National Development Fund and aims to achieve real estate development in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia by enabling beneficiaries to own suitable housing. This is achieved through the provision of financing solutions, programs and initiatives that keep pace with developmental transformations and growth requirements, contributing to the realisation of housing targets and Saudi Vision 2030.

Chairman's Message

H.E. Mr. Majed bin Abdullah Al-Hogail

Minister of Municipalities and Housing,
Chairman of the Board of Directors

Strengthening Financial Integration to Empower the Future of Housing

The year 2025 marked a significant turning point, as the Real Estate Development Fund (REDF) commenced the implementation of its updated corporate strategy designed to enable and drive the growth of the Saudi housing market. This is achieved through sustainable and innovative financial solutions that serve citizens and all market participants, contributing to enhancing the efficiency of the financing ecosystem, expanding the scope of benefits, and supporting the achievement of housing ownership targets.
By the end of the year, national homeownership reached 66.2%, steadily progressing toward the Vision 2030 target of 70%.

This milestone reflects not only increased access to housing but also the continuous development of a housing system designed to deliver wide ranging impact, capable of responding to economic cycles and demographic shifts, to meet the aspirations of the housing segment, including the youth.
While homeownership remains a deeply rooted aspiration, the society in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is characterized by its young population undergoing a qualitative generational shift. With growing numbers of citizens entering the housing market as first time buyers, homeownership is increasingly viewed not as an endpoint, but as a foundation for economic participation, stability, and long-term wellbeing.

The impact of homeownership extends beyond the household; it contributes to financial stability, limits exposure to rental market volatility, and enhances individual financial resilience. On a national level, the housing sector contributes to supporting the GDP, sustaining employment opportunities across various sectors, and reinforcing social cohesion.
In this context, housing is considered a social priority and a key strategic instrument of economic policy in the Kingdom for building a solid nation. Within this housing ecosystem, the Real Estate Development Fund (REDF) plays a pivotal role in delivering sustainable and innovative financial solutions.

Delivering Sustainable Homeownership for a New Generation

Delivering Sustainable Homeownership for a New Generation

In 2025, The Real Estate Development Fund (REDF) entered a new phase, placing sustainable and innovative financial solutions at the heart of its updated corporate strategy to drive the continued growth of the Saudi housing market. With national homeownership reaching 66.2%, REDF continues to make strong progress toward supporting the Kingdom's Vision 2030 target of 70%. During 2025, more than 83,000 housing finance contracts were enabled through REDF supported programmes, reflecting sustained demand and delivering meaningful impact in expanding access to homeownership.

REDF's role goes beyond direct beneficiary support, with a critical position as a key enabler of the financing housing ecosystem and this collaborative approach has translated into measurable outcomes. Institutional capability and operational excellence continue to be key enablers of our strategy. During the year, REDF advanced its digital and AI transformation agenda, with 84% usage of electronic services and Customer Satisfaction of digital services reaching 90%.

The seamless homeownership journey is enabled through The Real Estate Advisor platform with over 1.8 million registered beneficiaries. The platform allows beneficiaries to register, explore housing options and receive up to five home financing offers through a streamlined process completed in five simple steps. More than 710,000 beneficiaries were served through online and branch channels and Customer satisfaction (CSI), reached 86%, with a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 75%, reflecting growing trust in REDF's services and ease of beneficiary access.
