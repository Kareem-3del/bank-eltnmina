Housing Support Program

The Housing Support Program offers a streamlined experience for homeownership through integrated financing solutions, featuring non-refundable support packages worth 100,000 or 150,000 SAR based on income matrix. This is complemented by flexible financing options that meet beneficiaries' needs and accelerate their journey to their desired home with confidence and stability.

A supporting program aimed at reducing the burden of down payments required by financing entities when purchasing real estate through real estate financing. Promoting Social Stability for Women

The positive tangible impact of main support programs is evident in promoting social stability for women, particularly divorced and widowed women who serve as primary providers for their families. Through an integrated system of initiatives such as the Guarantees Program, Flexible Mortgage Program, Profit Reduction Program, and In-Kind Support, towards a total cumulative support 32.02% was directed to divorced women, while widows benefited from approximately 7.19% of the total support.
