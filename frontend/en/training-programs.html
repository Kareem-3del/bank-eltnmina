Exhibitions and Conferences:

The REDF strengthened its presence in the real estate and financing landscape through active participation in several specialized exhibitions and conferences. This diverse presence contributed to solidifying the REDF's image as a key partner in developing the real estate sector. It also opened broader channels for communication with beneficiaries, investors, and financiers, supporting the expansion of housing support programs and maximizing their developmental impact.

Certifications and Awards

The REDF's achievements began within its internal environment by obtaining a suite of international certifications reflecting governance maturity, procedural efficiency, and advanced digital services. These achievements then extended to local and regional awards that strengthened its position as a reliable developmental entity in the real estate financing market.

Arabian Best of Best Awards 2025

This institutional excellence was further crowned by a prominent presence on specialized award platforms, where the REDF won the Arabian Best of Best Awards 2025 for excellence in digital services. It achieved the award for the Best Entity in the Digitalization Category at the Kingdom level and the Best Entity in the Service Execution within Standard Time Category at the Middle East.
