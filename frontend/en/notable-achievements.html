Notable Works and Achievements

Highlights from communication campaigns

In 2025, the Real Estate Development Fund launched more than 16 diverse communication campaigns to raise awareness among beneficiaries of the housing support program about financing solutions and enablers that support homeownership.

Seven standout campaigns
  • Bayt Al-Omr (lifetime home)
  • Hiloul Tasandak (solutions that support you)
  • Solutions and options for homeownership
  • Early repayment service (Sudadh Hena)
  • Financing solutions and benefits (Tifarrq Ma’ak)
  • #فرصتك_وجتك_وأكثر
  • Awareness campaign: “Fhamak Kifaya” (understanding is enough)

Key agreements and memoranda of cooperation signed by the Fund

In 2025, the Real Estate Development Fund continued to broaden its partnerships with government, financial, investment, and technology stakeholders by signing a comprehensive set of agreements and memoranda of understanding spanning the housing value chain. These partnerships covered developing financing programs and additional support, financing off-plan sales projects, strengthening financial sustainability, activating digital solutions, exchanging and analyzing data, improving collection and operations efficiency, and building institutional and investment capabilities.

Information on the Fund’s financial performance

The Real Estate Development Fund maintained a strong capital base through the end of 2025, with authorized capital of about 190.9 billion , and paid-in capital of about 180.2 billion .

Financial sustainability

The Real Estate Development Fund advances financial sustainability through a renewed financing ecosystem built on diversifying revenue sources, creating real estate investment opportunities, and maximizing the efficiency of resource deployment. It also forges partnerships with the private sector, grows its investment portfolio within a risk-management framework, and improves operational spending efficiency—helping sustain its role in enabling households to own their homes.

Among the Fund’s most important initiatives in real estate investment are the following:

  • 1

    Creating investment opportunities with financial institutions across the Kingdom, with aggregate fund volume reaching SAR 1.21 billion . The funds are expected to help develop and deliver more than 1,500 housing units, supporting growth and sustainability of housing supply.

  • 2

    Launch of the supply chains product in the first quarter of 2025, aimed at supporting real estate developers by having suppliers settle payables for construction materials and supplies.

  • 3

    An agreement was signed to acquire the National Company for Supporting Financing Services, through the National Housing Company purchasing a share in the company—supporting institutional integration and strengthening the financing ecosystem.

  • 4

    An agreement was signed to manage an investment portfolio with Jadwa Investment, to steer investments toward high-impact real estate investment funds.

  • 5

    Engagement with 10 financial institutions to explore and present financing and real estate investment opportunities in the local market.

  • 6

    Work is underway with external asset managers to invest in real estate funds.

  • 7

    Cooperation with the Ministry of Housing to publish mortgage indicators for the Fund in the economic bulletin through the Balady platform.

  • 8

    Memoranda of understanding were signed with several financial institutions at Cityscape 2025, to strengthen cooperation in real estate investments and broaden available opportunities.

  • 9

    Supporting mortgage finance companies by providing supported liquidity totaling SAR 200 million , which resulted on average in 344 financing contracts at about SAR 581 thousand per contract.

  • 10

    A program to enable developers by supporting development companies: approved facilities totaling 958,375 million for more than +35 real estate projects, adding 4,586 housing units—boosting housing supply in the market and supporting homeownership goals.

Operating revenues (SAR millions)

Operating revenues exceeded the annual target, reaching about 949.3 million compared with a target of about 792.7 million , for an achievement rate of 120%.

Return on investment

The investment portfolio delivered a return of 5.11% in 2025.

Among the Treasury sector's key achievements in 2025:

  • Investment policy update project.
  • Signing agreements to manage real estate investment portfolios and private local equity portfolios.
  • Strengthening allocation in long-term strategic investments.
  • Spreading investment risk by increasing allocation in the local stock market.

Investment performance:

The investment portfolio achieved a return of 5.11% in 2025, compared with 5.42% in 2024, as the return declined in line with the decrease in interest rates on deposits and investment sukuk in financial markets.

Collection

To strengthen the Real Estate Development Fund's financial sustainability, 2025 focused on improving collection efficiency for the direct loan portfolio as a strategic pillar of financial performance. Accordingly, services for beneficiaries were developed, databases updated, and collection processes automated, alongside new payment channels, intensified collection campaigns, and the launch of the early repayment discount program. Together, these efforts increased collection rates, reduced default levels, and achieved the set targets.
